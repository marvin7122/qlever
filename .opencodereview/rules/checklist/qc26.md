# QC26: Every member that influences the computed result is reflected in `getCacheKeyImpl()`

## Statement
Every member that influences the computed result is reflected in `getCacheKeyImpl()`; descriptors/estimates are NOT part of the cache key. Non-cacheable results call `disableStoringInCache()` / override `canResultBeCached()` correctly. (see src/engine/Operation.h `getCacheKey`)
