# QC14: Classes/structs UpperCamelCase

## Statement
Classes/structs UpperCamelCase; locals & functions camelCase; member variables trailing underscore (`mode_`, `runtimeInfo_`); constants UPPER_SNAKE (`GEO_LITERAL_SUFFIX` in `src/global/Constants.h`) — no `k`-prefix constants.
