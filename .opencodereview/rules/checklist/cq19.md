# CQ19: Middle Man

## Statement
Middle Man: a new class/function that only forwards to another with no logic, validation, or added value — collapse the indirection or call the target directly. [Fowler, Refactoring 2nd ed: Middle Man]  (overlaps CQ3 for pure pass-through wrappers — report CQ3)
