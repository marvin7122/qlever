# CQ55: Constants centralized & documented

## Statement
Constants centralized & documented: new magic values (timeouts, limits, buffer sizes, counts) are named constants in one place with a comment on origin/justification, not scattered literals. [Kernighan & Pike, Practice of Programming ch. 1 (Style) / ch. 9 (Notation); Fowler, Refactoring 2nd ed: Magic Number]  (overlaps CQ12 — report CQ12 for a bare magic number)
