# CQ15: Shotgun Surgery

## Statement
Shotgun Surgery: one logical change in the diff forces small edits in >=4 unrelated functions/files (same concept/handler repeated across the change) — consolidate behind a single abstraction or helper. [Fowler, Refactoring 2nd ed: Shotgun Surgery; Ousterhout APoSD ch. 4]  (overlaps CQ1 — report CQ1 when it is literal copy-paste duplication)
